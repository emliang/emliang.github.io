LIANG Enming 梁恩明

Machine learning and optimization with hard constraints.

Research Assistant Professor City University of Hong Kong

I develop methods that respect constraints and scale to real decision-making systems, motivated by applications in power grids, mobility systems, and climate resilience. My work has appeared in Nature Energy, JMLR, ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, and AAAI. I am seeking a tenure-track Assistant Professor position at the intersection of ML/AI and sustainable systems. I welcome collaborations and discussions about research directions.

Background

Education. I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Data Science at City University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Minghua Chen; my dissertation received the 2026 ACM SIGEnergy Doctoral Dissertation Award Honorable Mention. I received my B.Eng. from SYSU, advised by Prof. Renxin Zhong.

Research & Community. I worked on the DeepOPF project with Prof. Minghua Chen and Prof. Steven Low of Caltech, and visited the University of Cambridge to work with Prof. Srinivasan Keshav on power-grid resilience under extreme weather. I led the 2024 and 2026 revisions of the AI for Optimal Power Flow tutorial for the Climate Change AI Summer Schools, with advisory comments from Prof. Priya L. Donti, Prof. Minghua Chen, and the CCAI Committee.

Industry. I collaborate with DiDi on decision-focused learning for urban mobility-on-demand systems, and previously interned at MSRA (Beijing, 2022) and Noah's Ark Lab (Shenzhen, 2021).

Teaching & Service. At CityU HK, I have served as sole instructor for SDSC3060 Operations Research and mentored Ph.D., master's, and undergraduate researchers on constrained generative modeling, graph neural networks for conic programming, and decision-focused learning (with joint work appearing at ICLR and AAAI). I serve as an Area Chair for ICLR 2027 and Program Committee Member for ACM e-Energy (2026–2027).
